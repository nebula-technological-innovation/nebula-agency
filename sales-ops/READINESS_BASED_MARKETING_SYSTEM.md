### Self-serve operator kits
Use one-time digital products as low-friction acquisition. Current price band: $29–$149.

## Preview/design-partner only
Aether/Orion/Atlas/Helix, VPN, cloud, mobile and other software candidates stay out of general-availability subscription marketing until production billing, entitlement, cancellation/refund, support and runtime evidence pass the fulfillment gate.

## Excluded from active conversion marketing
Supplier-dependent commerce without verified supplier/stock/cost/fulfillment evidence and regulated businesses without applicable provider/legal/compliance evidence.

## ICP routes
### Audit & de-risk
CTO, VP Engineering, founder, security lead, data lead.
Lead offers: strategy discovery, security assessment, data assessment.

### Build & automate
COO, technical founder, engineering lead, RevOps/operations lead.
Lead offer: engineering & automation sprint deposit.

### Grow & systemize
Founder, revenue leader, sales/marketing operations lead, agency owner.
Lead offer: B2B revenue operations sprint.

## Messaging formula
1. Name one operational problem.
2. Explain the consequence without invented numbers.
3. Describe the bounded deliverable.
4. State price/start point.
5. State what is explicitly not included.
6. Use one CTA.

## Sales cadence
- One current next action per account.
- No duplicate tasks.
- Do not send another follow-up while a prior follow-up window is still active.
- Replies outrank scheduled outreach.
- Personalize by verified account context; never invent pains, budgets, or initiatives.
- Sell professional services before unfinished SaaS.

## Referral motion
Position Nebula as a specialist layer for partners who need AI architecture, automation, defensive security, data integration, or governed agent systems. Do not compete for implementation work a partner is already contracted to deliver; define referral/co-delivery boundaries first.

## Commercial ladder
Operator kit → paid assessment → scoped implementation → support/managed operations.
Expansion requires proven customer need; no automatic upsell.

## Spend control
External spend remains $0 until verified realized revenue exists. After realization, marketing/operational reinvestment must remain within the global 15% realized-revenue cap.
