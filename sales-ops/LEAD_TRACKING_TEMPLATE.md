# Nebula Lead Tracking Template

## Google Sheets Structure

Create a Google Sheet with the following columns:

| Column | Type | Example | Notes |
|--------|------|---------|-------|
| Company | Text | ABC Roofing Co | Business name |
| Owner | Text | John Smith | Decision maker name |
| Email | Email | john@abcroofing.com | Primary contact email |
| Phone | Phone | (555) 123-4567 | Main business phone |
| Industry | Dropdown | Roofing | See industry list |
| Website | URL | www.abcroofing.com | Company website |
| Source | Dropdown | Google Maps \| LinkedIn | How we found them |
| Contacted | Date | 2026-05-29 | First contact date |
| Method | Dropdown | Email \| LinkedIn \| Call | Contact method used |
| Follow-up Date | Date | 2026-06-05 | When to follow up |
| Status | Dropdown | Lead \| Prospect \| Demo \| Client | Current status |
| Last Interaction | Date | 2026-05-30 | Most recent touchpoint |
| Notes | Text | [Long form notes] | Key details from conversation |
| Estimated Value | Currency | $2,500 | Projected contract value |
| Probability | % | 30% | Likelihood of closing |
| Next Action | Text | Schedule audit call | What's next |

---

## Status Definitions

- **Lead**: Identified but not yet contacted
- **Prospect**: Initial contact made, awaiting response
- **Contacted**: Responded to outreach
- **Demo**: Free audit scheduled or completed
- **Proposal**: Proposal sent, awaiting response
- **Negotiation**: Price/scope discussion
- **Client**: Project started
- **Retainer**: Recurring contract active
- **Closed-Lost**: Won't convert
- **Inactive**: No contact in 30 days

---

## Daily Workflow

### Morning (8am-11am)
1. Open lead tracking sheet
2. Identify 50 new leads from Google Maps / LinkedIn
3. Add to sheet with "Lead" status
4. Begin cold outreach

### Afternoon (12pm-4pm)
1. Review responses and schedule calls
2. Conduct discovery calls (update to "Demo" status)
3. Send follow-up emails
4. Create proposals for interested prospects

### Evening (5pm-8pm)
1. Update all notes and interactions
2. Schedule next follow-ups
3. Move prospects through pipeline

## Lead Sourcing Methods

### Google Maps Search Queries
```
- "dentists near me"
- "roofing companies near me"
- "real estate offices near me"
- "insurance brokers near me"
- "med spas near me"
- "law firms near me"
- "local moving companies"
- "marketing agencies near me"
```

### LinkedIn Search
- Search by industry + location
- Filter by company size (10-200 employees)
- Target decision makers (Owner, CEO, Operations Manager)

### Apollo.io
- Export leads by industry and location
- Automatically populate contact info
- Bulk upload to tracking sheet
