# Discovery Call Guide - 15 Minutes

## Pre-Call Prep (5 minutes before)
- [ ] Review company website
- [ ] Check their social media
- [ ] Identify potential pain points
- [ ] Prepare questions
- [ ] Test audio/video
- [ ] Have Calendly open for scheduling

---

## OPENING (1-2 minutes)

**Script**:
```
"Hi [Name], thanks so much for jumping on the call. I know you're 
busy, so I'll keep this brief.

I help businesses like [Business Type] automate repetitive workflows 
and improve operational efficiency. In our time today, I'd like to 
learn about your current operations and see if there are any quick wins 
we can identify.

Does that sound good?"
```

---

## DISCOVERY QUESTIONS (6-8 minutes)

### Question 1: Current Workflow
**"How do you currently manage [X process]?"**
- Listen for: Manual steps, multiple tools, handoffs
- Follow-up: "How long does this take per day/week?"

**Examples**:
- "How do you manage customer intake?"
- "How do you handle appointment scheduling?"
- "How do you track and follow up with leads?"
- "How do you manage customer communications?"

### Question 2: Pain Points
**"What's the biggest challenge with this process?"**
- Listen for: Time, errors, missed follow-ups, customer satisfaction
- Follow-up: "How much time does this cost you weekly?"

### Question 3: Current Tools
**"What tools are you currently using?"**
- Listen for: Disconnected systems, outdated tools, multiple platforms
- Follow-up: "Do these tools talk to each other?"

### Question 4: Security/Compliance
**"How do you currently manage security and access control?"**
- Listen for: MFA, password management, compliance needs
- Follow-up: "How often do you review access?"

### Question 5: Decision Making
**"What would success look like for you?"**
- Listen for: Time savings, revenue growth, team capacity
- Follow-up: "How would you measure improvement?"

---

## CLOSE (2 minutes)

### If Interested:
```
"Great! I'd like to send you a proposal with specific recommendations.

What's the best way to stay in touch - email or phone?

I'll have something to you by [DAY], and we can schedule a follow-up 
call to review."
```

### If Not Interested:
```
"I totally understand. If your situation changes or you decide to 
prioritize this, feel free to reach out anytime.

I'll send you some resources that might be helpful in the meantime."
```

---

1. **Update CRM**:
   - Status → "Demo" or "Follow-up"
   - Next action → "Send proposal" or "Schedule follow-up"
   - Notes → Key takeaways from conversation
