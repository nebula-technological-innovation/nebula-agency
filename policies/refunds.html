Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective date: September 13, 2026

Business identity. Nebula Technological Innovation is an operating technology business of Roe Acquisitions LLC. This policy describes the default customer process. Product-specific checkout terms, order forms, supplier terms, or signed statements of work may provide different or more specific rights. Applicable law always controls where it requires otherwise.

1. Subscription services

You may cancel a recurring subscription to stop future renewals using the cancellation method provided for the product or by contacting customer support. Unless the applicable plan states otherwise, cancellation does not automatically reverse charges already incurred for a billing period or usage already consumed.

2. Professional services

For discoveries, assessments, sprints, implementations, and pilots, refund eligibility depends on whether work has begun, work already performed, reserved capacity, non-cancellable third-party costs, and the governing statement of work or checkout terms. If you need to cancel, contact us promptly so we can determine the completed and unperformed scope and any amount eligible for refund.

3. Digital products

If a digital product is not delivered, is materially defective, or you are charged more than once for the same purchase, contact us for correction. Because digital products may be delivered immediately, completed delivery may affect refund eligibility.

4. Physical products

Returns and warranties for physical technology products are governed by the product-specific terms shown at purchase and any applicable manufacturer, distributor, or authorized reseller conditions. Contact us before returning an item so the correct return path can be confirmed.

5. Duplicate or unauthorized charges

If you believe a charge is duplicated, incorrect, or unauthorized, contact us promptly with the transaction date, amount, and the email used for the purchase. Do not send full card numbers or sensitive authentication information by email.

6. How to request help

Contact christianroe@roeacquisitions.net and include your name, purchase email, product or service, transaction date, and a short description of the issue. We will review the request against the applicable purchase terms, delivery evidence, and legal requirements.
