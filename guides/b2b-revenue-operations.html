More outreach does not fix a broken revenue process.

Before increasing volume, verify that the target account, problem, offer, next action, and conversion measurement are coherent. Otherwise scale mostly amplifies noise.

Diagnostic questions

  • Is the ICP specific enough to disqualify bad-fit accounts?
  • Does each outbound message map to one plausible buyer problem and one offer?
  • Does every account have exactly one next action?
  • Are replies, opt-outs, recent touches, and stale opportunities reconciled before follow-up?
  • Can you measure qualified replies, purchases, fulfilled revenue, refunds, and expansion separately?
