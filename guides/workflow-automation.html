Nebula Decision Guide

Automate repeatable work, not unresolved chaos.

The best automation candidates are frequent, rules-driven, observable, reversible, and expensive enough to justify maintaining the integration.

Diagnostic questions

  • Is the workflow repeated often enough to matter?
  • Are inputs and outputs structured or reliably detectable?
  • Is there a clear exception path and human owner?
  • Can every external side effect be made idempotent?
  • Can the workflow be measured before and after automation?

When a paid engagement makes sense

Use an engineering sprint when the workflow already has a clear owner and outcome but needs API/MCP integration, event routing, CI/CD, cloud work, or production hardening.
