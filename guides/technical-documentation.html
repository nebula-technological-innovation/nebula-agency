Documentation debt becomes operational debt.

Missing documentation usually surfaces as slower onboarding, repeated questions, fragile deployments, inconsistent incident response, and knowledge trapped with one person.

Diagnostic questions

  • Could a new engineer deploy or roll back without asking the original author?
  • Are system boundaries and dependencies documented?
  • Do APIs, MCP tools, credentials, and operational ownership have clear references?
  • Are incident, hotfix, and recovery steps written and testable?
  • Can business operators understand what happens when automation fails?

When a paid engagement makes sense

Use a documentation sprint when the missing knowledge is known but needs to be turned into a coherent package of runbooks, SOPs, architecture docs, specifications, or operating handbooks.
