Before you automate, identify the actual architecture problem.

Teams often reach for an AI agent, workflow tool, or new platform before separating four different issues: unclear business ownership, missing system boundaries, poor integration design, and genuine model capability gaps.

Diagnostic questions

  • Can you name the business outcome and owner?
  • Is there one authoritative source for each critical record?
  • Are authentication, permissions, auditability, and rollback defined?
  • Can the workflow fail closed without creating financial or customer harm?
  • Is AI necessary for the uncertain step, or would deterministic automation be better?
