Nebula Decision Guide

Production security starts with evidence, not assumptions.

A useful defensive review should identify exposed secrets, dependency risk, configuration weaknesses, identity boundaries, logging gaps, and the remediation order without performing unauthorized or destructive testing.

Diagnostic questions

  • Are secrets excluded from source and rotated through approved storage?
  • Can you produce an SBOM and identify vulnerable dependencies?
  • Are production identities least-privilege and separated from development?
  • Are sensitive actions authenticated, authorized, logged, and replay-safe?
  • Do you have rollback, incident, and credential-revocation procedures?
