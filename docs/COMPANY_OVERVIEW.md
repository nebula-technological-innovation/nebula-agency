## Company description

Nebula Technological Innovation is a founder-led technology business based in Colorado. The company is building an AI-native software portfolio centered on business workflow automation, multi-model AI infrastructure, developer tooling, and secure operational systems.

The **AetherTech AI** family encompasses AI model access and application experiences. **Atlas** focuses on agent coordination and governed execution. **Aether IDE** and **Helix** address developer workflows. Related infrastructure and research efforts include security tooling, Nebula cloud and operating-system concepts, and Aether Lens prototypes.

Nebula's commercial approach is to start with practical, scoped problems: discovery, assessments, bounded implementations, and carefully validated recurring operations. Product capabilities are not all at the same readiness stage. Proposed, implemented, tested, deployed, and generally available are separate statuses requiring distinct evidence.
